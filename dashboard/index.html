Who generated it: energy by owner and station

Utility-scale only: rooftop solar is not in AEMO's dispatch data, so renewable shares are lower than figures that include it. Renewables = solar, wind, hydro and bioenergy; batteries ("Grid") are storage and count on neither side. Capture price = generation-weighted price the fuel earned in its region (5-minute prices for ranges up to 30 days, daily averages beyond). Capacity factor = energy / (registered capacity × hours), over units that generated in the range. Net exports = AEMO's net interconnector flow out of each region. Beyond 30 days the price heatmap and the average day are hourly, over the whole months the range touches. The dashed Demand line on the generation chart is AEMO's operational demand (it excludes rooftop solar); with a region selected, the gap between it and the stack is roughly that region's net exports. Batteries: the average day of the battery fleet, output above the axis and charging below, with the price it saw; "bought" and "sold" are the prices weighted by the energy charged and discharged (5-minute ranges only, since a day's total nets one against the other). Who generated it: energy by owner and power station, coloured by fuel; click an owner to zoom in. All charts follow the header filters.
