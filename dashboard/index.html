Utility-scale only: rooftop solar is not in AEMO's dispatch data, so renewable shares are lower than figures that include it. Renewables = solar, wind, hydro and bioenergy; batteries ("Grid") are storage and count on neither side. Capture price = generation-weighted price the fuel earned in its region (5-minute prices for ranges up to 30 days, daily averages beyond). Capacity factor = energy / (registered capacity × hours), over units that generated in the range. Interconnector flows are AEMO's 5-minute dispatch targets for the last 14 days; arrows point the way power flows, thickness is the flow and colour how close it runs to its limit. All charts follow the header filters.
